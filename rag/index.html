Information goes in. It doesn’t come back out.

Every day, your business creates knowledge: contracts, invoices, intake forms, emails, scans, approvals. Most of it lands where it stops being useful: an inbox, a shared drive, a system nobody searches.

Those places are digital black holes. Search can’t find what fell in, and AI can’t use it. Ask an AI about your own business and it either admits it doesn’t know, or invents an answer and delivers it with total confidence.

That’s not an AI problem. That’s a data access problem.

The pipeline that feeds the engine

MaxxLogix designed its documents and data ingestion around a true RAG engine (retrieval-augmented generation). From the moment information arrives, it is processed to become retrievable, in six steps:

1

Intake

Documents and data arrive from every source (paper, scans, email, forms, uploads) through one front door.

2

Chunking

Content is broken into meaningful pieces, each holding one complete idea.

3

Embeddings

Each piece becomes a vector: numbers capturing meaning, so similar ideas sit close.

4

AWS OpenSearch

Vectors are indexed in AWS OpenSearch, each tied to its source.

5

Semantic search

Questions find meaning, not just keywords. Your question is vectored too; the closest pieces come back.

6

Grounded answers

Answers are built from your data, with sources: the document, the clause, the page. Not imagination.

This pipeline runs inside Outcome Cloud™. IDP Outcomes™ feeds it at intake, and MaxxOwl™ SI works on what it retrieves.

Episode 1: Your Company’s Knowledge Is Trapped

Episode 1 opens on the black hole itself. Your company already has the answers; they’re trapped in inboxes, shared drives and silos, so an AI asked about your own business can only guess. The fix is RAG: before answering, the AI retrieves the relevant passages from your documents and data and builds its answer from those. MaxxLogix was built the other way around: ingestion designed around the engine, so information is retrievable from the moment it arrives.
